Biography

  • Profession: Singer-songwriter, AI researcher, educator
  • Education: B.S in Psychology, Yonsei University; M.S and PhD from KAIST Graduate School of Culture Technology
  • Current Affiliation: Antenna Music; Adjunct Professor at Yonsei University's Underwood International College
  • Music Career:
    • EP "DIARY" (2008), albums "Saebyeokbyeol" (2010), "High Heel" (2012), "Ballades Op.3" (2019)
    • Singles "I Will Sing" (2013), "SKY HIGH" (2015)
    • EP "Everblooming" (2023)
    • Album "Breath, Still" (2025)
    • Composed hit songs for Jeong Seung-hwan, Lyn, Kwon Jin-ah, Park Won, and Lee Seok-hoon
  • Media Roles:
    • Hosted "Nanjang" (Gwangju MBC, 2009-2010)
    • DJ on EBS "Beautiful World of Children’s Songs" (2011-2012)
    • Music teacher on EBS's "Ding-dong-dang Kindergarten" (2020-2021)
  • Academia:
    • Former adjunct professor at Baekseok and Hongik University
